About the company

A small, director-led company focused on business support

OSF Holdings Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 13189815. It was incorporated on 9 February 2021, and its registered business activity is other business support services (SIC 82990).

The company offers general support with the everyday work that keeps an organisation moving: routine administration, organisational tasks and practical help with projects and operations. It is aimed at businesses and organisations that need an extra pair of hands without taking on more than they need.

With a single director at its head, the company is deliberately small. That keeps communication simple and direct, with one point of contact from the first conversation onwards.
